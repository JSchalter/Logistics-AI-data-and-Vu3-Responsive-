ASK LOGISTICS — EXAMPLE QUESTIONS
==================================

These questions are designed for the Ask Logistics panel. Answers are grounded
only in retrieved historical records. The application does not claim live GPS,
traffic, weather, pricing, carrier-feed, or routing information.


GENERAL EXPLORATION
-------------------
- What patterns appear in the most relevant logistics records?
- Summarize the most relevant shipment evidence for this question.
- Which source datasets are represented in the retrieved evidence?
- What lanes are most common in the relevant records?
- What shipment statuses appear most frequently in the retrieved evidence?
- Which fields in the evidence are most useful for understanding this question?


DELAYS AND SERVICE PERFORMANCE
------------------------------
- What delay patterns appear in shipments similar to this one?
- What is the average and median delay in the most relevant records?
- Which relevant records show the largest delay hours?
- Are delayed shipments concentrated on particular lanes in the evidence?
- What do the relevant records suggest about planned ETA versus actual delay?
- Which carriers appear most often among the retrieved delayed shipments?
- Are there signs of repeated delay patterns in the relevant route records?
- Summarize the delay-risk and service-deviation context in the retrieved data.
- What is the relationship between traffic level and delay in the relevant records?
- What is the relationship between weather severity and delay in the relevant records?


CARRIER COMPARISON
------------------
- Compare carrier cost, transit time, and delay patterns in the relevant shipments.
- Which carriers are most represented in the retrieved evidence?
- How do the relevant carriers compare on observed shipping cost?
- How do the relevant carriers compare on observed transit days?
- Which carrier records show the strongest on-time or delayed pattern in this evidence?
- What historical carrier reliability context is available in the relevant records?
- Are some carriers associated with more exception statuses in the retrieved data?
- Compare UPS, FedEx, DHL, USPS, OnTrac, and other carriers represented in the evidence.


COST AND TRANSIT
----------------
- What shipping-cost range appears in the most relevant records?
- What is the average and median shipping cost in the relevant evidence?
- What transit-day range appears for similar shipments?
- Which lanes have the highest observed cost in the retrieved data?
- Which relevant records have the shortest observed transit time?
- Which relevant records have the longest observed transit time?
- Is there an apparent relationship between distance and shipping cost in these records?
- Is there an apparent relationship between distance and transit days in these records?
- Compare cost-per-shipment patterns for the relevant carriers or lanes.


ROUTES, LANES, AND NETWORK CONTEXT
----------------------------------
- Which origin-to-destination lanes are most common in the retrieved records?
- What is the evidence for the MIA to Detroit lane?
- What delay, cost, and transit patterns appear for this route?
- Which routes have the highest route-risk scores in the relevant evidence?
- Which records have high traffic or weather severity on similar lanes?
- Are there recurring route identifiers in the retrieved records?
- What origin and destination combinations appear most often in this evidence?
- Summarize the operational context for shipments on this lane.


RISK, DISRUPTION, AND OPERATIONS
--------------------------------
- What risk classes appear in the retrieved evidence?
- What is the average route-risk score among the relevant records?
- What disruption-likelihood values appear in similar shipments?
- Which records have the highest disruption likelihood?
- What operational factors are present in the highest-risk retrieved records?
- What detention or dwell-time patterns appear in the relevant records?
- Are there links between route risk, traffic level, and delay in this evidence?
- What supplier-reliability context is available in the retrieved records?


DATA QUALITY AND ANOMALIES
--------------------------
- Are there date-quality flags in the relevant records?
- Do any retrieved records show transit-date mismatches?
- Are missing cost or missing delivery-date issues represented in this evidence?
- Which evidence rows need manual data-quality review?
- Are there extreme cost values in the most relevant records?
- What fields are missing or incomplete in the retrieved evidence?


EVIDENCE-FOCUSED FOLLOW-UPS
---------------------------
- Cite the record IDs that support your answer.
- What can be concluded from the evidence, and what cannot?
- Is the retrieved evidence sufficient to answer this question?
- Which two records best illustrate the observed pattern?
- Summarize only the facts present in the returned evidence rows.
- Do not assume live conditions; explain the historical pattern only.


PREDICTION QUESTIONS — USE THE DEDICATED PANELS
------------------------------------------------
For a new shipment prediction, use the Tracking Intelligence or Carrier
Recommendation panels rather than Ask Logistics. Those panels call the
validated sklearn models and return governed predictive outputs.

- Will this shipment be delayed? Use Tracking Intelligence.
- What is the predicted delay probability or delay hours? Use Tracking Intelligence.
- What will this US shipment cost? Use Carrier Recommendation or the US API.
- How many days will this US shipment take? Use Carrier Recommendation or the US API.
- Which carrier should I choose for cheapest, fastest, reliable, or balanced? Use Carrier Recommendation.
